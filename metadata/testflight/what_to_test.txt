Train Libre 1.3.0-alpha.2 — please focus on the new diary weight entry:

1. LOG WEIGHT FROM THE DIARY
- Open today in the Diary and use the Weight card below Supplements.
- Tap “Log”, move the ruler between values, then save. Confirm that the movement feels smooth and the saved measurement appears immediately.
- Cancel an edit and confirm that the previous value remains unchanged.

2. EMPTY AND EXISTING DAYS
- Open today before adding any diary data. The usable Diary and Weight card should still appear instead of the no-data placeholder.
- Verify that the card shows the latest earlier measurement and its age until today receives its own measurement.
- After logging today’s weight, tap the card and confirm that it opens the existing measurements history.

3. UNITS AND PRESENTATION
- Switch between metric and imperial units in Settings. The card and ruler should display kg or lb correctly while preserving the same saved measurement.
- Check that the Weight title, value, unit, age label, buttons, and chevron match the typography and colours of the surrounding Diary cards.

Thanks for testing Train Libre!
