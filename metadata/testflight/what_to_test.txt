Train Libre 1.2.1 — please focus on testing the following improvements and fixes:

1. MACRONUTRIENT RECOMMENDATIONS (Adaptive Nutrition)
- Check your nutrition recommendations in Nutrition -> Goals / Settings: Fat target is now anchored per kilogram (0.9 g/kg on a cut, 1.0 g/kg at maintenance/bulk) rather than a flat 0.6 g/kg minimum, providing a more balanced fat/carb split.
- Verify that recalculating or applying goals updates your macronutrient targets as expected.

2. CAFFEINE & SUPPLEMENT TRACKING
- Log caffeine via drinks/fluids or food items and confirm it appears correctly in:
  • The Diary daily summary and fluids section
  • The Supplement overview screen
  • The caffeine progress bar and daily limit
- Check that existing custom supplements remain intact.

3. CARD MORPHING & TRANSITION ANIMATIONS
- Tap cards across the app (Workout History logs, Routine exercises, Food Explorer, Supplement Hub, Diary meal cards) to open and close them.
- Check that the container morph animation opens and collapses smoothly without double-card artifacts, flickering, or flashing lists.

4. TELEMETRY CONSENT SHEET
- On a fresh install or onboarding reset, verify that optional telemetry consent is asked in a dedicated bottom sheet after the mandatory privacy consent.
- Check that your choice is properly reflected in Settings -> Privacy & Telemetry.

Thanks for testing Train Libre!
