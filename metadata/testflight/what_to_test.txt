Train Libre 1.3.0-beta.1 — please focus on supersets:

1. BUILD A SUPERSET IN A ROUTINE
- Open a routine, switch to edit mode and use the link action at the end of an exercise's "Add set" row to join it with the exercise below.
- Confirm that both cards share one coloured bracket and are labelled A1 and A2.
- Join a third exercise into the same group, then separate the middle connection. The two remaining pairs should stay supersets instead of the whole group dissolving.
- Drag a member of a group to another position. The whole group should move together and stay contiguous.
- Delete one exercise of a two-exercise group and confirm the group is dissolved.

2. TRAIN A SUPERSET
- Start a workout from that routine and tick off a set of A1. No rest timer should start; the next open set of A2 should come into focus.
- Tick off the matching set of A2. The rest timer should start with the pause configured on the last exercise of the group.
- Finish the final round and confirm that no rest timer starts.
- Try a group whose exercises have different set counts. The leftover sets of the longer exercise should behave like a normal single exercise.
- Force-quit the app mid-workout and reopen it. The session should come back with its groups intact.

3. HISTORY AND EXISTING DATA
- Open a finished superset workout in the history. Members should be bracketed together and labelled A1/A2, and connections should be editable there as well.
- Log the same exercise twice in one workout as two separate blocks. The history should show two separate cards, not one merged card.
- Open older workouts recorded before this build. They should look and behave exactly as before.

4. BACKUP ROUND TRIP
- Create a backup, restore it, and confirm that supersets and repeated exercise blocks survive both in routines and in workout history.
- Share a workout as text and check that group members are marked A1/A2.

5. REGRESSION CHECK
- Confirm that training volume, estimated 1RM and personal records are unchanged for workouts that contain no supersets.

Thanks for testing Train Libre!
