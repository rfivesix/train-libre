Train Libre 1.5.0

Please focus your testing on adaptive goals, workout plans, and new tracking features:

• Adaptive Nutrition Goals & Weekly Reviews: Create a weight goal from a real weight measurement (in onboarding or Profile -> My Goals), inspect its trajectory, and complete a weekly review. Confirm that a recommendation does not change calorie or macro targets until you explicitly apply it.
• Training Experience Levels: Select Beginner, Advanced, or Pro in Settings or onboarding, and check the adjusted workout interface, muscle anatomy details, and RIR controls.
• Manual Workout Plans & Reminders: Build both a fixed weekly plan and a flexible sequence from saved routines. Include a rest day, enable reminders, start the due workout from the Diary, and verify complete, partial, and skipped states.
• Timed Sets & Live Activities: Start a workout, tap the play/stop timer on set rows, and verify pause/resume from the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity.
• Weight Trend Smoothing & Health Import: Check the metabolic trend smoothing curve with touch points in Measurements and Home Screen widgets. Test importing body measurements under Settings -> Health & Tracking.
• Workout Hub & Routines: Check routine ordering after creating, editing, and starting a routine. Test edit, duplicate, share, delete, quick start, and workout history.
• Nutrition Statistics: Inspect several time ranges, navigate between periods, and press or drag across macro bars to verify the selected day's values.

Please also report any localisation, accessibility, notification timing, backup/restore, layout, or general stability issues.

Thank you for testing Train Libre!
