Train Libre 1.5.0

Please focus your testing on adaptive goals, manual workout plans, and the redesigned Workout tab:

• Adaptive Nutrition Goals: Create a weight goal from a real weight measurement, inspect its trajectory, and complete a weekly review. Confirm that a recommendation does not change calorie or macro targets until you explicitly apply it.
• Manual Workout Plans: Build both a fixed weekly plan and a flexible sequence from saved routines. Include a rest day, start the due workout from the Diary, and verify complete, partial, and skipped states. A missed sequence workout should remain next; no catch-up workout should be added.
• Workout Reminders: Enable a plan reminder, change its time, and verify that opening the notification leads to the active plan. Retiring or replacing the plan should remove obsolete reminders.
• Workout Hub & Routines: Check routine ordering after creating, editing, and starting a routine. Test edit, duplicate, share, delete, quick start, workout history, and exercise catalogue navigation.
• Nutrition Statistics: Inspect several time ranges, navigate between periods, and press or drag across macro bars to verify the selected day's values.

Please also report any localisation, accessibility, notification timing, backup/restore, layout, or general stability issues.

Thank you for testing Train Libre!
