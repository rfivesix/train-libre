Train Libre 1.2.0 — please focus on the following.

1. AI MEAL CAPTURE (Nutrition tab -> AI capture)
Needs your own API key: Settings -> AI Meal Capture -> provider -> paste key.
- Take one to four photos of a plate and check the recognised foods, amounts
  and macros on the review screen. Correct, swap and remove items before saving.
- Hold the camera on a packaged product: a barcode chip should appear in the
  same screen, without switching modes. Try the barcode toggle next to the
  shutter as well.
- Import photos from the library instead of taking them.
- Leave the analysis screen while it is running: the late result must be
  discarded, not pushed at you afterwards.

2. VOICE DICTATION
Hold the microphone button and describe a meal — on its own and in addition to
a photo. Check that the transcript is editable before sending, and whether the
sheet tells you when recognition falls back to the network recogniser.

3. LiDAR SCALE (iPhone Pro models only)
Photograph the same meal once with and once without "Send LiDAR scale" in the
AI settings, and compare the estimated portion sizes. Also try clearly too
close (< 15 cm) and too far (> 120 cm) — you should get distance guidance only
in those cases.

4. WORKOUT PHOTOS
Add up to 4 photos to a workout, both in the summary right after training and
later in the workout history. Delete single photos, page through the carousel,
and confirm the photos survive a backup and restore.

5. iCLOUD BACKUP AND RESTORE
Let the automatic backup run, then restore on a device that already has data.
Expected: no restart needed, meal and workout photos come back with their
entries, the diary, profile and supplements keep working right after the
restore, and onboarding still asks for region, catalogs and permissions.

6. DIARY LAYOUT
A scanned meal stays one entry with its photo and unfolds into its ingredients.
Entries are sorted by calories, largest first, and amounts and calories should
line up in the same columns on every row.

7. RUNNING WORKOUT BAR
Start a workout, leave the screen and watch the bar above the tab bar: status
dot, elapsed time, and the switch to rest with the remaining pause, plus the
exercise of the next open set. Tapping it should morph the workout screen out
of the bar; the chevron in the header collapses it back. Watch for flicker of
the bar at the start and end of the animation, and check the discard button
still asks for confirmation.

Thanks for testing!
