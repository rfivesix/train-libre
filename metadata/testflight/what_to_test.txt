Train Libre 1.7.0-beta.1

Please prioritise these checks and report crashes, lost data, incorrect totals, or changes made without approval. Include your device, iOS version, steps, and screenshots where helpful.

1. Update and data safety: Back up an existing 1.6.0 installation, then update to the beta. Confirm the app starts and your diary, workouts, plans, goals, settings, and photos are intact after restarting. Check the What's New and AI introduction. If possible, restore the backup on a test device and verify the same data.

2. AI permissions and changes: Ask the assistant about your diary or training. Deny access first, then approve only the requested data and check the answer against the app. Create or revise a routine, plan, or recipe: discard one proposal and apply another, verifying nothing changes before approval and the applied result persists. Try follow-up questions, chat history after restarting, and hiding/reopening the assistant.

3. Meals and macro totals: Scan or describe a mixed meal and a drink, review and edit ingredients, portions, liquid amount and caffeine, then save. Compare diary and daily calorie/macro totals; edit, move and delete entries. Also try manual macro entry and an AI chat meal proposal. Cancel a scan and confirm the camera and diary remain responsive. Test camera, photo attachment and voice on a physical iPhone if available.

4. Apple Health nutrition: With permission, import daily nutrition, refresh twice, and check totals and duplicates. Edit or delete an imported entry and refresh again; local changes should remain. Export a manual macro entry and check the result in Health. Try denying permissions as well.

5. Core flows and setup: Complete a workout and restart the app to verify sets are saved. Try a fresh install, onboarding, optional AI/analytics consent, and offline startup. Test Spanish or Dutch, catalog search, and custom colours in light and dark mode if available.

Thank you for testing Train Libre!
