Dear App Review Team,

Train Libre 1.7.0 is a private, open-source workout and nutrition tracker. Core logging works without an account, subscription, or purchase. The app is available in English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Spanish, and Dutch. The 1.7.0 store listing also includes Spanish and Dutch localizations.

REVIEW PATHS

1. Complete onboarding with an age of at least 16. Health permissions and optional usage statistics are not required to use the core app.

2. Workout logging: Open Workout to start a session or create a routine. Log sets, finish the session, and review the saved workout and training history. Routines and training plans can be created and edited without an account.

3. Nutrition logging: Open Diary, tap +, then choose add Food to search for foods or enter a meal. Core food logging does not require AI. The downloadable BLS food catalog can be searched offline after installation; Open Food Facts provides branded-product data when online.

4. AI Meal Capture: Enable AI Features in Profile > Settings > AI Features. From Diary, tap + > add Food and use the AI action beside the food search field. Capture a meal photo, enter or dictate a description, and review the suggested foods and portions before saving. Barcodes can also be scanned from the capture screen. On supported iPhones, Apple Intelligence can analyse meals on device without an API key. Other providers require the user to configure their own API key. AI features are optional.

5. AI Chat: Open AI Chat from the Add menu. The assistant can answer questions and prepare reviewable drafts for meals, routines, training plans, and recipes. When a request needs personal app data, the app presents the requested data scope for approval. Proposed changes are not saved until the user reviews and applies them.

6. Health and recovery: Open Statistics to review workout and recovery summaries. Profile > Settings > Health contains optional Apple Health or Health Connect permissions and nutrition sync controls. Health access is requested only through the system permission flow.

7. Data and backups: Profile > Settings > Data backup & import opens the Data Hub. Users can create or restore a backup and import supported workout exports. Import previews are shown before changes are applied.

PRIVACY AND HEALTH CONTEXT

Core workout and nutrition data is stored locally. AI requests are sent only to the provider selected by the user; the selected provider receives the content needed for that request. Apple Intelligence meal analysis runs on device on supported iPhones. Personal app data is shared with AI Chat only after user approval. Health access and usage statistics are optional; usage statistics are off by default and do not contain health data. Train Libre has no ads, IDFA access, or cross-app tracking.

Train Libre is for personal fitness logging. Nutrition, recovery, sleep, and physical metrics are for general training guidance, not medical diagnosis or treatment.
