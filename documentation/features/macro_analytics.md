# Macronutrient Analytics

The Statistics hub includes a Nutrition section for reviewing calorie and macro intake over time. The overview card follows the selected date range and period; opening it carries those filters into the dedicated macro statistics view.

## Time ranges and navigation

The chart supports 7-day, 1-month, 3-month, 6-month, 1-year, and maximum-history ranges. Previous and next period controls move within the selected range, and the date picker lets users choose an anchor period. The seven-day chart can select individual days; longer ranges summarize denser history for readability.

## Intake detail

The detail view summarizes calories, protein, fat, and carbohydrates and presents daily intake chronologically. In the seven-day chart, selecting a day's bar updates the selected-day values. In longer ranges, the chart prioritizes trend comparison over dense per-day labels.

The Nutrition hub also shows a rolling seven-day comparison between logged intake and the current target. Recommendations remain separate from this history view and do not change targets unless the user applies them.
