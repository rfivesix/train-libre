# Manual Workout Plans

Manual plans arrange saved routines into a schedule without creating workout debt or catch-up sessions.

## Plan formats

- **Weekly plan:** Assign routines or rest days to weekdays. A missed weekday does not move the remaining schedule.
- **Training sequence:** Arrange routines and rest days in a repeating sequence of 1–14 days. If a workout is missed, it remains next until it is completed or explicitly skipped.

Only one manual plan is active at a time. Plans retain routine snapshots and revisions so later routine edits do not rewrite the historical prescription. The plan overview shows the full schedule and the relevant current or next slot.

## Session tracking

Users can start a due planned session directly from the Diary or plan view. The plan records completed, partially completed, and skipped sessions. Completed and partial entries can open the saved workout details. A skipped session is explicit; the app does not add an extra workout to compensate for a missed day.

## Reminders and data portability

Optional workout reminders are scheduled locally for planned workout days at the time selected in Settings. Opening a reminder routes to the active plan. Updating, replacing, or retiring a plan reschedules or removes reminders tied to the previous plan. Plans are included in app backup and restore.
