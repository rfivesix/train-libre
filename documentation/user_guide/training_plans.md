# Create and follow a training plan

Create a weekly schedule or repeating sequence from your saved routines, then start and review planned sessions.

The built-in AI assistant can prepare a new plan or a revision from your saved routines. Approve access to the relevant plan and routine data when asked, review the proposed days and routines, and apply the draft only when it is correct. New plans are saved inactive; revisions to an active plan preserve the current cycle by default.

## Create a plan

1. Open **Workout** and tap the **Plan** card. If you have no plan yet, tap **Create**; otherwise tap the plus button in the plan screen.
2. Enter a plan **Name**, then choose **Week** or **Sequence**:
   - **Week** assigns routines to fixed weekdays and repeats every week. Missed days stay on their original weekdays.
   - **Sequence** repeats a day-by-day order of 1–14 days. A missed workout remains next until you complete, partly complete, or explicitly skip it. New sequences start with three days; use **Add day** or the remove and drag controls to change the length and order.
3. Tap each weekday or sequence day, then choose a saved routine or **Rest**. A plan needs at least one routine.
4. Tap **Save and activate**. The plan becomes active and is ready to follow.

You can keep multiple saved plans, but only one can be active. To switch plans, open **Saved plans** in the plan screen, choose an inactive plan, then choose **Resume** or **Start over**. To pause the active plan, open **Manage plan** and choose **Deactivate**. Its history remains available.

## Follow the schedule

1. Open **Workout** and tap the **Plan** card, or open the planned session card in the **Diary**. The plan screen shows the schedule and a selectable week calendar; tap a date to see its routine and status.
2. On a due workout day, tap **Start** or **Start workout**. The usual live workout screen opens with the planned routine. For instructions on recording sets and finishing, see [Track a workout](tracking_a_workout.md).
3. To leave a due workout uncompleted, tap **Skip** and confirm. It is recorded as skipped and the plan moves on.
4. Completed and partly completed sessions show their outcome on the plan calendar. Tap **View workout** to open the saved workout details. A session is partly completed when you save a started workout without completing all prescribed sets; extra sets are retained too.

For a past planned workout, the app asks you to confirm **Make up this past planned session now?** The workout starts now but remains assigned to its original planned day. Missed sessions do not create catch-up workouts automatically.

## Add rest days and edit a plan

Choose **Rest** for a day while creating or editing the schedule. Rest days appear in the plan but do not have a workout to start or skip.

To edit a plan, open **Manage plan** and choose **Edit**. When saving an edit, choose whether it takes effect **Today** or at the **Next cycle**. The plan keeps past session instructions as they were when those sessions were planned. If you edit a routine from a plan day, you can choose **Change routine only** or **Update plan too**.

## Set workout reminders

1. Open **Profile** > **Settings** > **Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Remind me on workout days**, then choose **Reminder time**.

Reminders are optional and scheduled on your device for upcoming workout days in the active plan. Rest days and missed past sessions do not generate reminders. Allow Train Libre to send notifications in your device settings for reminders to appear. Pausing or changing the active plan updates the scheduled reminders.

For the scheduling and session outcome rules in more detail, see [Manual Workout Plans](../features/manual_workout_plans.md).
