# Create and reuse recipes

Save a combination of foods as a recipe, then log a portion to your Diary whenever you eat it.

The built-in AI assistant can prepare or revise a recipe using foods from Train Libre's local catalog. Approve access to saved recipes when asked, review the proposed ingredients and amounts, and apply the draft only when it is correct. External AI assistants do not have access to this local catalog, so their ingredient data must be checked during the app's import preview.

## Create a recipe

1. Open **Nutrition** and find **My recipes**, or open **Nutrition Explorer** and select **Recipes**.
2. Tap **Create recipe**. You can also save foods already listed together in a Diary meal: expand **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner**, or **Snack**, then tap **Save as recipe** below the entries.
3. Enter a **Recipe name**. In the editor, tap **Add ingredient**, choose a food, enter its **Amount** in grams or milliliters, and tap **Add**. Repeat for each ingredient.
4. Optionally enter **Servings in recipe**, **Finished weight after cooking**, and **Notes**. The finished weight helps scale a portion by cooked grams; servings let you log a fraction of the recipe.
5. Tap **Save**. A recipe needs a name and at least one ingredient.

Ingredient search uses the foods available in Train Libre. For help finding or creating individual food entries, see [Log food and review calories and macronutrients](logging_food.md).

## Edit, duplicate, or delete a recipe

1. Open **Nutrition Explorer** and select **Recipes**, or locate the recipe under **Nutrition > My recipes**.
2. Open a recipe to review its ingredients and nutrition totals. Tap **Edit** (the pencil) or use the recipe’s options menu and choose **Edit recipe**.
3. In edit mode, change the recipe name, serving count, cooked weight, notes, or ingredient amounts. Use **Add ingredient** to add foods; remove an ingredient with its delete control. Tap **Save**.
4. To make a copy or remove a recipe, open its options menu and choose **Duplicate recipe** or **Delete recipe**, then confirm deletion if prompted.

## Log a saved recipe

1. From **My recipes** or the **Recipes** tab, tap **Add food** on the recipe. Alternatively, open the recipe and tap **Add food**.
2. In the confirmation sheet, choose the date, time, and meal type. If the recipe has serving information, adjust **Servings eaten**; you can also adjust the total amount and ingredient amounts.
3. Tap **Save** to log the ingredients in **Diary**. Each ingredient is recorded as food for the selected meal, grouped under the recipe name.

The recipe remains available for future logs. Changes to a recipe template do not rewrite food entries already recorded in your Diary. For ordinary food logging and daily totals, see [Log food and review calories and macronutrients](logging_food.md).

## Share or import a recipe

Use the recipe’s **Share** option to share it. To add a shared recipe, open **Nutrition Explorer > Recipes** and tap the import icon in the top bar. Importing from this list adds the recipe to your collection. Importing while editing an existing recipe replaces that recipe’s contents. For AI photo, voice, and barcode capture, see [Meal Capture Pipeline](../features/meal_capture_pipeline.md).
