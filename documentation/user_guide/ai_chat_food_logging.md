# Log food with the AI chat

Use the floating AI assistant to turn a written meal description or an attached
photo into a diary entry. The assistant creates a review draft in the chat; it
never adds the entry until you apply it.

## Add a meal

1. Open the AI assistant and describe what you ate. You can attach one or more
   meal photos to the same message.
2. Ask explicitly to log it, for example: “Log this as dinner” or “Add this
   meal to today's diary.” A photo by itself or a question about its contents
   does not create a diary entry.
3. Review the meal draft shown below the assistant's reply. Check the meal name,
   date and meal period, each food name and portion weight, and the estimated
   calories and macros.
4. Choose **Apply** to save it or **Discard** to leave the Diary unchanged.

The review card uses the same expandable draft and confirmation flow as other
AI-created items. Each listed food becomes a macro-only diary entry, so the
assistant does not need to find a matching item in the food catalog. Protein,
carbohydrates, and fat are stored per 100 g; calories are calculated with the
app's existing 4/4/9 factors. Sugar, fiber, salt, and caffeine are included
when the estimate provides them.

## Photos and corrections

When you apply a photo-based draft, Train Libre copies the message's attached
photos into the meal's permanent diary storage. The saved meal keeps working if
you later remove the chat. Discarding the draft does not add a meal photo to
the Diary; the attachment remains part of the chat until that chat is removed.

Photo and text estimates can be wrong. Review portion sizes and nutrition
before applying. After saving, open the meal in the Diary to edit its name,
time, foods, amounts, or nutrient values; you can also use **Open** on the
saved proposal card. For a catalog-backed entry with a
barcode or saved product, use the regular food search flow described in
[Log food and review calories and macronutrients](logging_food.md).

Your selected AI provider receives the text and any photos used to prepare the
draft. See [Configure and use AI Meal Capture](ai_meal_capture.md) and
[Share Train Libre data with an AI assistant](sharing_and_ai.md) for provider
and privacy information.
