# Read and customize the Statistics hub

Use **Statistics** to review activity, recovery, body, training, and nutrition. Choose which cards appear first, reorder them, or open the all-data view to see every available statistic, including cards with no records yet.

## Choose and arrange statistics

1. Open **Statistics**. The hub shows a fixed recent period for each card.
2. Tap the sliders button in the top bar to open **Edit Statistics**.
3. In **Pinned**, drag the handle beside a card to change its order. Tap the minus button to remove a card from the hub.
4. Under **More Statistics**, choose a card from its category to add it to **Pinned**. Your selection and order are saved automatically. Tap **Done** when you finish.
5. Back in the hub, scroll to **Show All Data** to open every card grouped by category, even when it has no data. This view does not change your pinned selection.

## Choose a period

The hub uses a fixed timeframe appropriate to each card. Open a card to explore other periods using the controls in its detail screen. The summary tiles use their own labeled lifetime or 30-day periods.

## Available cards

Cards are grouped under **Activity**, **Recovery**, **Body**, **Training**, and **Nutrition** in **Edit Statistics** and **Show All Data**. The hub can show these cards:

- **Activity:** **Steps** appears when step tracking is enabled. Its large count is for **Today**, with the last seven days in the chart. Tapping it opens **Steps** at today’s **Day** view. See [steps and health data](health_data.md#find-imported-data).
- **Recovery:** **Sleep score**, **Pulse**, and **Muscle Readiness**. Sleep and pulse depend on their tracking options. The sleep and pulse cards open their detail views at today; Muscle Readiness opens the recovery tracker. See [steps, sleep, and pulse](health_data.md#find-imported-data) and [training statistics](training_statistics.md).
- **Body:** **Body & Nutrition** uses the last 30 days and opens a detail view with that period selected. **Body measurements** is a shortcut to Measurements, which has its own date range. See [Body & Nutrition statistics](body_nutrition_statistics.md) and [record body measurements](measurements.md).
- **Training:** **Training Rhythm**, **New Personal Bests**, and **Muscle Groups**. The hub summary for Training Rhythm is fixed to **Last 6 weeks**, and Muscle Groups to **Last 8 weeks**. New Personal Bests uses the last 30 days. Their detail screens have separate date controls. See [training statistics](training_statistics.md).
- **Nutrition:** **Nutrition** summarizes calories and macronutrients; **Water** summarizes logged fluid; **Micronutrients** summarizes vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients from BLS catalog foods logged in the Diary. Nutrition and Micronutrients use the last seven days; Water uses today. Each opens its own detail view. See [Body & Nutrition statistics](body_nutrition_statistics.md), [micronutrients](#review-micronutrients), and [log water and drinks](water_and_drinks.md).

## Review micronutrients

Add **Micronutrients** from **Edit Statistics** if it is not already pinned, then tap its card. Choose **1D**, **7D**, **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y**, or **MAX** in the detail view. Use the arrows to move between periods or tap the date label to select a day. The summary shows averages per logged day for multi-day periods, the number of days with BLS foods, and the number of BLS food entries. Nutrients are grouped by type; each row includes its unit and the number of days with a recorded value. Tap the share icon to share the displayed summary as text.

Only foods from the BLS catalog are included in these calculations. Other catalog foods, branded products, and custom foods do not contribute, even if they contain nutrient values. A period with no logged BLS foods shows an empty-state message. Nutrient details are unavailable until the BLS catalog is installed; see [food catalog settings](food_catalogs.md#update-the-databases-manually) to update databases. The displayed averages describe logged days and are not a complete estimate of every day in the selected period.

## When a card has little or no data

Some cards depend on tracking being enabled; others need recorded workouts, body measurements, or Diary entries. Cards without records explain what is missing and can still be opened for more detail. **Show All Data** keeps every statistic visible so you can find a card before it has records. If an enabled health card is empty, check its access and tracking settings in [Health settings](health_data.md#connect-a-health-source). If a workout or nutrition card is empty, log the relevant data and revisit Statistics.

The hub is a summary. For individual charts, confirm the displayed period and units on the detail screen before comparing values. To share selected records or summaries with another app, including an AI assistant, see [Share Train Libre data with an AI assistant](sharing_and_ai.md).
