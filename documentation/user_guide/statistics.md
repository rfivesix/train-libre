# Read the Statistics hub

Use the **Statistics** hub to scan your workout, body, nutrition, and enabled health summaries, then open a card for more detail.

## Choose a period

1. Open **Statistics**. The hub starts with **7D** selected.
2. Choose **7D**, **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y**, or **MAX**. This changes the period shown by the hub cards that follow the selected range.
3. For a period other than **MAX**, use the arrows beside the date label to move backward or forward. Tap the date label to choose a date and either a rolling period or a calendar period. The date picker starts at 2020. Forward navigation stops at the current period.
4. Tap a card to open its detail screen. Check that screen’s date controls: several detail views have their own period and do not follow the hub selection.

**MAX** shows the available history for that view and has no date navigation. A detail screen may offer different ranges from the hub.

## Scan the sections and cards

- **Steps** appears when step tracking is enabled. Its card shows the selected-period label and step count. With **7D** selected, the large count is for **Today**; with another hub range selected, it is **Total steps** for that range. Tap the card to open **Steps**, which starts at today’s **Day** view regardless of the hub range. See [steps and health data](health_data.md#find-imported-data).
- **Recovery** appears when sleep or pulse tracking is enabled. It contains **Sleep score** and/or **Pulse**, depending on which tracking options are on. These cards show the hub period label, but tapping opens the current day in the separate Sleep or Pulse view. See [steps, sleep, and pulse](health_data.md#find-imported-data).
- **Body** contains **Body & Nutrition** and the **Body measurements** shortcut. The summary card follows the hub’s selected period and opens its own detail view with that period selected. The shortcut opens **Measurements**, which has a separate date range. See [Body & Nutrition statistics](body_nutrition_statistics.md) and [record body measurements](measurements.md).
- **Training** contains **Training Rhythm**, **New Personal Bests**, and **Muscle Groups**. Their hub summaries do not all use the selected range: Training Rhythm shows **Last 6 weeks**, and Muscle Groups shows **Last 8 weeks**. **New Personal Bests** follows the selected range. Tap a card for its detail screen; the ranges and controls differ by screen. See [training statistics](training_statistics.md).
- **Nutrition** shows a macronutrient summary for the selected hub period. Tap it to open the Nutrition detail view, which has its own range controls. See [Body & Nutrition statistics](body_nutrition_statistics.md) and [Macronutrient Analytics](../features/macro_analytics.md).

## When a card has little or no data

Some cards depend on tracking being enabled; others need recorded workouts, body measurements, or Diary entries. A card with no data can show an empty overlay over a faint example layout. Treat only unobscured values as your records. If an enabled health card is empty, check its access and tracking settings in [Health settings](health_data.md#connect-a-health-source). If a workout or nutrition card is empty, log the relevant data and revisit Statistics.

The hub is a summary. For individual charts, confirm the displayed period and units on the detail screen before comparing values.
