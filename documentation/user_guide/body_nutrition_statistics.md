# Read Body & Nutrition statistics

Use **Body & Nutrition** to review weight and logged calorie trends for a period, then compare them with the data coverage shown on the screen.

## Open the detail view and choose a period

1. Open **Statistics** and tap the **Body & Nutrition** card under **Body**. The detail view starts with the period selected in the hub.
2. In the detail view, choose **7D**, **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y**, or **MAX**. Use the arrows beside the date label to move between periods, or tap the label to choose a rolling or calendar period.
3. Read the **Body & Nutrition** summary: **Current Weight** is the latest weight recorded in the selected period; **Weight Change** summarizes change across that period; **Avg. Calories** is the average on days with logged calories. The two trend cards show **Rising**, **Falling**, **Stable**, or **Unclear** for weight and calories.
4. Check **Effective range**, the confidence label, and **Based on … weigh-ins and … calorie days** before interpreting the trends. Missing values show as a dash, and insufficient or sparse entries can produce **Unclear** or an insufficient-data message.

## Read the trend chart

Under **Weight and calories over time**, use the legend to identify the weight and calorie series. The chart scales each series to fit the same space, so compare their directions and dates, not their vertical magnitudes. Tap a chart point to see its raw weight or calorie value in the selected unit and kcal. **Interpretation** repeats the observed pattern, confidence, and data coverage.

This is trend context; it does not show that calorie changes caused weight changes. Log body weight in [Measurements](measurements.md) and food in [the Diary](logging_food.md) to build the two series. Body weight imported from Apple Health or Health Connect can also appear; see [health data](health_data.md).

## Review Nutrition statistics

The **Nutrition** card on the Statistics hub opens the **Nutrition** detail view with the hub’s selected period. Use the detail view’s own **7D**, **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y**, or **MAX** controls to change the period. For periods other than **MAX**, use the arrows beside the date label or tap the label to choose a rolling or calendar period. The summary shows period-average calories and macronutrients by default; tap a day on the chart to show that day’s values. Scroll to the daily breakdown to review dates with logged intake. For more about chart selection and totals, see [Macronutrient Analytics](../features/macro_analytics.md).

In the current English app, parts of this screen still display hard-coded German labels, including **Makronährstoffe** and **Tagesverlauf**. The **Nutrition** app-bar title and the chart data remain available.

The Statistics hub also has a separate **Micronutrients** card for vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients from BLS catalog foods. Its data coverage and limits are described in [Read and customize the Statistics hub](statistics.md#review-micronutrients).
