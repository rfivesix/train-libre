# Get started with Train Libre

Set up your profile, choose how Train Libre displays information, and find the app’s main sections.

## Complete the initial setup

1. On the welcome screen, choose **Set Up Profile**. Use **Next** and **Back** to move through setup; the final button is **Start Tracking**.
2. Choose **Metric** (kg, cm, ml) or **Imperial** (lbs, in, fl oz), choose your food database region, and select a **Training experience** level: **Beginner**, **Advanced**, or **Pro**. **Beginner** keeps the interface focused on essentials such as weight and reps and uses everyday muscle names; **Advanced** adds anatomical muscle breakdowns; **Pro** adds RIR intensity logging and cardio intensity zones.
3. Enter your date of birth and gender, height, and current weight. Body fat is optional. The app requires users to be at least 16 years old.
4. Continue through **Current Activity** and the nutrition-goal pages. You can skip setting a personal nutrition goal; if you set one, review the recommendation before continuing. You can change daily targets later in [Daily Targets](daily_targets.md) and manage a weight goal in [My Goals](weight_goals.md).
5. On the workout-history page, import a supported file or continue without importing. You can import later from Settings; see [Import workout history and other data](importing_data.md).
6. Review the health-permission choices and enable the connections you want. You can change them later under **Profile > Settings > Health**; see [Connect health data](health_data.md).

If you are restoring data, choose **Restore from Backup** on the welcome screen. On Apple devices, **Restore from iCloud** appears when a backup is available. A restore replaces the data on the device; see [Import workout history and other data](importing_data.md#restore-a-train-libre-backup).

## Edit your profile

1. Tap the profile button in the top-right corner of a main screen to open **Profile**.
2. Tap the profile card to edit your name, date of birth, or gender. Tap your profile picture to add or change it. Height is collected during setup but is not editable from this profile dialog.
3. Tap **Save**.

## Change app preferences

1. From **Profile**, tap **Settings**.
2. Tap **Appearance**. Choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. You can also turn **Haptic feedback** on or off. On Android, **Material colors** switches between dynamic system colors and Train Libre’s brand accent.
3. Under **Unit System**, choose **Metric (kg, cm, ml)** or **Imperial (lbs, in, fl oz)**. Train Libre uses the selected system for supported measurement displays and entry fields.
4. Under **Additional Nutrient in Overview**, choose **Fiber**, **Sugar**, or **Salt** for the extra nutrient tile in the Diary overview.
5. Under **Training experience**, choose **Beginner**, **Advanced**, or **Pro**. Beginner focuses on essentials such as weight and reps and uses everyday muscle names; Advanced shows anatomical muscle breakdowns; Pro adds RIR (reps in reserve) for lifts and intensity zones for cardio. This changes how training details are presented and logged. Under **Training progression**, choose **Off** or **Suggest**. **Suggest** can prefill eligible workout set weights and repetitions from your history; see [Workout progression](../features/workout_progression_engine.md) for how suggestions work.
6. Open **Calculation basis** to set **Baseline daily activity** and **Extra cardio/endurance outside the app**, then tap **Save**. Baseline activity is separate from extra cardio. Extra cardio covers endurance exercise not logged as Train Libre workouts. These settings inform the adaptive nutrition recommendation; see [Adaptive nutrition goals](../features/adaptive_nutrition_goals.md) for details.

## Find the main sections

1. Use the bottom navigation bar to open **Diary**, **Workout**, **Statistics**, or **Nutrition**.
2. Tap the profile button at the top right to open **Profile**, where you can reach Settings and your profile information.
3. Tap the **+** button beside the bottom navigation bar for shortcuts to common actions, including adding food or a measurement and starting a workout.

You can change these preferences later in **Settings**. Changing the unit system does not require repeating setup.
