# Review workout history

Find a completed session, review its summary and records, or correct its details.

## Find a completed workout

1. Open **Workout** and tap the calendar icon in the top bar to open **Workout History**. You can also open history from the workout card in **Diary**.
2. Scroll the list, which shows completed workouts with the newest first. Each entry shows its routine name (or **Free Workout**), start date and time, total lifted volume, set count, and duration when available.
3. Tap an entry to open **Workout Details**.

If history is empty, finish a workout to add the first entry. To import past sessions, see [Import workout history and other data](importing_data.md).

## Read workout details

The summary bar shows duration, total lifted volume, and set count. Scroll to review the exercises and their recorded sets. When a session includes a new record, it appears under **New Records**. Heart-rate information appears only when Train Libre can read matching workout heart-rate data; it may be unavailable if permission or usable samples are missing. Workout photos appear when the session has photos.

## Edit workout details

1. In **Workout Details**, tap **Edit**.
2. Tap the date and time row to choose a new date and time. If the workout has an end time, tap **Duration** to change or clear the duration.
3. Edit **Notes** or workout photos if needed. Use **Take photo** or **Choose from library** to add photos, or **Remove photo** on a displayed photo. Photo changes are saved when you choose the photo action.
4. Tap **Save** in the top bar to save the workout date, duration, notes, and exercise changes.

To change exercises, sets, or superset links, see [Supersets and edit workout entries](supersets_and_editing.md).

## Save, share, or delete a workout

- To make a reusable routine from the session, tap **Save as Routine**, enter a routine name, and confirm. See [Create and use workout routines](workout_routines.md).
- To share the session, tap the share icon in **Workout Details**. Choose **Share as image**, **Share as formatted text**, or **Export as JSON / data**, then follow the sharing options shown.
- To delete a session, swipe its entry in **Workout History** and confirm the deletion. Deleting removes the completed workout from history.
