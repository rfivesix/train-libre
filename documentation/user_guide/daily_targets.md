# Set daily targets

Set the calorie, nutrient, water, and step targets that Train Libre uses to show progress in your Diary and step summaries.

1. Open **Profile** and tap **Daily Targets** (labeled **Tagesziele** in the German UI).
2. In **DAILY GOALS** (or **TAGESZIELE**), enter values for **Calories**, **Protein**, **Carbs**, **Fat**, **Water**, and **Steps**. Use calories for the energy target, grams for the nutrient targets, milliliters (or fluid ounces) for water, and a step count for steps.
3. In **DETAILED NUTRIENTS**, enter targets for **Sugar**, **Fiber**, and **Salt** if you want to track them.
4. Tap **Save**. Train Libre saves the values and returns to the previous screen.

The Diary compares logged calories, macros, water, and the selected detailed nutrient with these targets. Step summaries use the step target. For logging foods and reading the Diary totals, see [Log food and review calories and macronutrients](logging_food.md).

**Daily Targets** are the values used for daily progress. They are separate from **My Goals**, where you manage a body-weight goal and its trajectory. A weekly adaptive review can recommend different calorie and macro targets, but it does not change your active targets until you choose to apply the recommendation. See [Adaptive Nutrition Goals](../features/adaptive_nutrition_goals.md).
