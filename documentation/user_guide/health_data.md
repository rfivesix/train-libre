# Connect health data

Connect Apple Health or Health Connect to view steps, sleep, pulse, and supported body measurements in Train Libre.

## Connect a health source

1. Open **Profile** > **Settings** > **Health**. On iOS, the page heading is **Apple Health**; on Android, it is **Health Connect**.
2. Turn on the switches for the data you want Train Libre to read. Each switch requests the corresponding permission from Apple Health or Health Connect. Approve access in the system prompt.
3. If a permission was denied or changed later, review Train Libre’s read access in Apple Health or Health Connect settings. The status below each switch shows whether access is ready, partial, denied, or unavailable.
4. To refresh records manually, use the refresh action beside **Enable steps tracking**, **Enable sleep tracking**, or the body-measurement option. The action labels are **Sync steps now** and **Import sleep data now**; body measurements use **Import now**.

Train Libre reads data from the health store on the device. Steps, sleep, pulse, and nutrition are opt-in separately. Enabling body-measurement import requests access and imports available records; it reads weight and body fat on both platforms, and waist measurements from Apple Health. Train Libre skips its own measurement records. Health Connect may provide only recent body-measurement history unless full-history access is available.

## Import nutrition totals

In **Profile > Settings > Health**, enable **Import nutrition** and grant the requested health-store access. Daily nutrition totals are imported as separate Diary entries; pull to refresh in Diary to request updated totals. Manual entries remain separate, so check for overlapping intake before interpreting your daily totals. If you edit an imported day locally, later imports do not overwrite that local edit; deleting it prevents the importer from recreating it. Health export is a separate setting.

## Find imported data

- **Steps:** Open **Diary** and choose a day. The Steps card appears when there are step records for that day. In **Statistics**, the **Steps** card appears when step tracking is enabled. Tapping it opens **Steps** at **Day** for today, regardless of the hub period. In **Steps**, choose **Day**, **7D**, or **1M**; the date controls and picker move this view independently. **Day** shows an hourly timeline, total, active hours, and peak hour. **7D** shows daily steps for the week. **1M** shows daily steps for the month and a summary with average per day and goal progress. The source name and last update may appear below the chart. Step counts and the goal line depend on available records and your step goal.
- **Sleep:** Open **Diary** and choose a day with sleep data, or open **Statistics** > **Recovery** > **Sleep score**. The hub card summarizes the current day; it does not pass the hub’s selected period into Sleep. In **Sleep**, choose **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**, then use the date label, arrows, or date picker to select a calendar period. On **Day**, tap **Duration**, **Heart rate**, **Regularity**, **Depth**, or **Interruptions** to open that metric’s detail chart. Week and Month summarize scored nights and let you select a day to open its daily view. Missing sleep records, unavailable permissions, or too little data can leave metrics unavailable or show an empty-state message. For how the score is calculated, see [Sleep scoring](../features/sleep_scoring_engine.md).
- **Pulse:** Open **Statistics** > **Recovery** > **Pulse**. The hub card uses the selected hub period as its label, but the **Pulse** detail screen opens at **Day** for today. Choose **Day**, **Week**, or **Month** and use the date controls to change its period. The screen shows **Range**, **Average**, and **Resting** in bpm; its chart shows samples over the day or resting values over the week/month. Sample count and a data-quality label help you judge sparse coverage. If there are too few samples, the chart may explain that it cannot show a reliable chart; if there are no samples, the screen explains whether tracking is disabled, access is denied, or the platform is unavailable. Resting pulse is an estimate for tracking, not a medical measurement.
- **Body measurements:** Imported measurements appear in **Statistics** > **Body measurements**. This opens **Measurements** with its own date range, independent of the hub selection. For charts and editing measurement entries, see [Record body weight and other measurements](measurements.md).

For the full hub range behavior and its other cards, see [Read the Statistics hub](statistics.md). For weight and calorie comparisons, see [Read Body & Nutrition statistics](body_nutrition_statistics.md).

Train Libre checks for new steps and sleep records when health data is loaded in Diary, and may refresh them when the previous sync is at least six hours old. **Sync steps now** refreshes step records; **Import sleep data now** requests up to 365 days of sleep history. Pulse is read for its analysis view when **Enable pulse analysis** is on; there is no separate pulse import button. For workout, sleep, and activity records written from Train Libre to the health store, see [Exporting and backing up data](exporting_and_backing_up.md#exporting-to-apple-health-or-health-connect) and [Health sync and export](../features/health_sync_export.md). For other supported imports, see [Import workout history and other data](importing_data.md#import-health-data).
