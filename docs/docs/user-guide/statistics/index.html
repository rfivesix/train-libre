Read and customize the Statistics hub

Use Statistics to review activity, recovery, body, training, and nutrition. Choose which cards appear first, reorder them, or open the all-data view to see every available statistic, including cards with no records yet.

Choose and arrange statistics#

  1. Open Statistics. The hub starts with 7D selected.
  2. Tap the sliders button in the top bar to open Edit Statistics.
  3. In Pinned, drag the handle beside a card to change its order. Tap the minus button to remove a card from the hub.
  4. Under More Statistics, choose a card from its category to add it to Pinned. Your selection and order are saved automatically. Tap Done when you finish.
  5. Back in the hub, scroll to Show All Data to open every card grouped by category, even when it has no data. This view does not change your pinned selection.

Choose a period#

  1. Choose 7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, or MAX in the hub. Cards that use the hub period update to that selection.
  2. For a period other than MAX, use the arrows beside the date label to move backward or forward. Tap the date label to choose a date and either a rolling period or a calendar period. The date picker starts at 2020. Forward navigation stops at the current period.
  3. Tap a card to open its detail screen. Check that screen’s date controls: several detail views have their own period and do not follow the hub selection.

MAX shows the available history for that view and has no date navigation. A detail screen may offer different ranges from the hub.

Available cards#

Cards are grouped under Activity, Recovery, Body, Training, and Nutrition in Edit Statistics and Show All Data. The hub can show these cards:

  • Activity: Steps appears when step tracking is enabled. With 7D selected, its large count is for Today; with another hub range selected, it is Total steps for that range. Tapping it opens Steps at today’s Day view. See steps and health data.
  • Recovery: Sleep score, Pulse, and Muscle Readiness. Sleep and pulse depend on their tracking options. The sleep and pulse cards open their detail views at today; Muscle Readiness opens the recovery tracker. See steps, sleep, and pulse and training statistics.
  • Body: Body & Nutrition follows the selected hub period and opens a detail view with that period selected. Body measurements is a shortcut to Measurements, which has its own date range. See Body & Nutrition statistics and record body measurements.
  • Training: Training Rhythm, New Personal Bests, and Muscle Groups. The hub summary for Training Rhythm is fixed to Last 6 weeks, and Muscle Groups to Last 8 weeks. New Personal Bests follows the selected hub period. Their detail screens have separate date controls. See training statistics.
  • Nutrition: Nutrition summarizes calories and macronutrients; Water summarizes logged fluid; Micronutrients summarizes vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients from BLS catalog foods logged in the Diary. These cards use the selected hub period and open their own detail views. See Body & Nutrition statistics, micronutrients, and log water and drinks.

Review micronutrients#

Add Micronutrients from Edit Statistics if it is not already pinned, then tap its card. Choose 1D, 7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, or MAX in the detail view. Use the arrows to move between periods or tap the date label to select a day. The summary shows averages per logged day for multi-day periods, the number of days with BLS foods, and the number of BLS food entries. Nutrients are grouped by type; each row includes its unit and the number of days with a recorded value. Tap the share icon to share the displayed summary as text.

Only foods from the BLS catalog are included in these calculations. Other catalog foods, branded products, and custom foods do not contribute, even if they contain nutrient values. A period with no logged BLS foods shows an empty-state message. Nutrient details are unavailable until the BLS catalog is installed; see food catalog settings to update databases. The displayed averages describe logged days and are not a complete estimate of every day in the selected period.

When a card has little or no data#

Some cards depend on tracking being enabled; others need recorded workouts, body measurements, or Diary entries. In the regular hub, cards can show an empty overlay over a faint example layout. Treat only unobscured values as your records. Show All Data keeps every statistic visible so you can find a card before it has records. If an enabled health card is empty, check its access and tracking settings in Health settings. If a workout or nutrition card is empty, log the relevant data and revisit Statistics.

The hub is a summary. For individual charts, confirm the displayed period and units on the detail screen before comparing values. To share selected records or summaries with another app, including an AI assistant, see Share Train Libre data with an AI assistant.
