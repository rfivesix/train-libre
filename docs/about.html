The person behind the project

A training app shaped by real use.

I started Train Libre because I wanted to build a real software project and because I wanted a better way to follow my own training. I enjoy going to the gym and want to make progress, but the apps I tried did not show me the metrics I cared about. I did not want to pay for a pile of features I would never use, or turn a training log into another social platform. I wanted to log a workout quickly, see the numbers that matter to me, and export my data whenever I choose.

I want Train Libre to stay a tool. No ads, no gamification, no social feed, and no server I have to run to use my own data. I decide what the app should solve, which features matter, and how the screens and everyday workflows should feel. I work with AI coding agents in natural language, explain what I want to achieve, and think through the options with them. They write most of the code. I rarely write code myself, but the product decisions, priorities, and final call on whether something works are mine.

I study Computer Science at TU Berlin. Data analysis and the structured way software projects are developed have influenced how I think about features and how to decide whether they are finished. I also bring my own experience with training and nutrition. Before I test a change myself, I run automated tests to make sure new work does not break existing features. Then I try it in the simulator with different test data, install it on my own device, and listen to feedback from friends who use the latest beta.

Release history

The app, release by release.

The releases that brought meaningful changes to how Train Libre works for you.

  1. September 2025

    v0.1.0

    A solid starting point for everyday tracking

    Log workouts, food, and body measurements. Keep an eye on consistency and protect your records with encrypted backups.

  2. September 2025

    v0.2.0

    More confidence during a workout

    Your active session now survives closing the app, and each set can show what you did the last time.

  3. September 2025

    v0.3.0+2

    Get back to the workout you were doing

    Fixed workout restoration and navigation so an interrupted session is easier to pick up again.

  4. December 2025

    v0.4.0

    Meals and drinks in the same diary

    Group foods into meals, track water and caffeine, and move through a redesigned interface.

  5. March 2026

    v0.5.0

    Cardio joins strength training

    A rebuilt setup flow and support for cardio exercises make the app work for more kinds of training.

  6. March 2026

    v0.6.0

    Less searching when logging food

    Capture a meal with AI and get meal or drink suggestions based on your remaining nutrition targets.

  7. March 2026

    v0.7.0

    A clearer view of your progress

    Explore workout consistency, personal records, muscle distribution, recovery, and body trends in one statistics hub.

  8. April 2026

    v0.8.0

    Understand adaptive calorie targets

    See uncertainty ranges and diet phases behind recommendations, then choose when to apply a change.

  9. May 2026

    v0.9.0

    Train Libre brings the essentials together

    Workout routines, nutrition, hydration, supplements, measurements, statistics, and backups come together in one offline-first app.

  10. August 2026

    v1.0.0

    A more polished day-to-day experience

    Refined workout editing, exercise reordering, and scrolling across the app to make common tasks feel smoother.

  11. August 2026

    v1.1.0

    Your workout stays visible beyond the app

    Follow a live workout from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island, and reach useful tracking views with home-screen widgets and shortcuts.

  12. August 2026

    v1.2.0

    Log a meal from a photo

    Capture food with the camera, barcode, or photos, review the suggested entries, and attach photos to completed workouts.

  13. September 2026

    v1.3.0

    Supersets and more accurate exercise logging

    Link exercises into supersets, log bodyweight and assisted movements more accurately, and track bodyweight from the diary.

  14. September 2026

    v1.4.0

    Guidance for what to do next

    Opt into editable next-set suggestions, then review personal records, muscle groups, and a year of training rhythm in statistics.

  15. October 2026

    v1.5.0

    Long-term goals with a visible path

    Set a nutrition and weight goal, follow your progress against a target pace, and choose the training experience that fits you.

  16. October 2026

    v1.6.0-beta.1

    Plans, imports, and data you can move

    Build weekly training plans, import workout history, and share plans, routines, recipes, and workouts. A downloadable food catalog also brings generic foods into offline search.
