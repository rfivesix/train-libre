Train Libre is an open-source, offline-first workout and nutrition tracker that keeps you in control of your data. Log workouts and meals without an account. Your data is stored locally on your device.

FEATURES
- Log workouts set by set, build routines and training plans, and follow your progress and personal records.
- See completed and planned sets by muscle group and training volume during a workout.
- Track meals, calories, macros, fluids, caffeine, supplements, bodyweight, and measurements. Enter macros manually or search food catalogs.
- Explore training, nutrition, sleep, steps, pulse, and recovery statistics where data is available.
- Optionally sync supported health data, including nutrition, with Health Connect.
- Use optional AI meal analysis and chat with your own provider credentials. Review suggestions before saving and approve requests for personal app data.
- Export and restore local backups.

Train Libre is open source, has no mandatory cloud account, and is built for structured training. Optional usage statistics are off by default.

Train Libre is not a medical app. Recovery, sleep, and other physical metrics are for personal training guidance, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

The app uses community catalogs including Open Food Facts and OpenExerciseDB. Source code is publicly available.
